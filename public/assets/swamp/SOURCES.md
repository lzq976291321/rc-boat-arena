## Integration notes

The active boat is [Futuristic Speedboat by dylanheyes](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/futuristic-speedboat-5a8f4d21472e4b2b8ec4d23f70cf8cbc), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). File: `../library/futuristic-speedboat/model.glb`; 162,766 triangles, 7,687,736 bytes. The original GLB and six 2K textures remain unchanged. Runtime changes center the source, rotate its +X bow to -Z, scale length to 5.2 m (width 1.418 m), place the keel 0.28 m below water before bobbing, and align the wake to the stern. The old boat lamps and their water glow were removed. Navigation remains the existing calm wetland exploration. Full file credit and checksum: [SOURCES.md](../library/futuristic-speedboat/SOURCES.md).

The tree is a broadleaf island tree used as a wetland visual stand-in, not a botanically exact cypress. Near trees reuse the scanned geometry; distant trees use original procedural geometry. Water, sky, fog, wakes, water lilies and ambient water audio are generated by this project.

`asset-manifest.json` includes original URLs, license URLs, SHA256 hashes, dimensions and transformations.

Visual reference: [Chetan Ankola / Stillwater](https://x.com/chetanankola/status/2101595285675970853). This project uses an independent implementation and no proprietary Stillwater code or assets.

## Racing shore reuse — 2026-09-22

Island Tree 02 and Fern 02 are also used by the Songlan Lake race scene. The original GLB files and public URLs are unchanged. The racing scene owns its parsed geometries and materials, scales and grounds each instance, and batches planting by spatial sector. Source credits and licenses above apply to both scenes.
